Job Title: Business Analyst

Company Overview:
We are a global consulting firm that helps organizations optimize their operations, improve efficiency, and drive growth through data-driven insights and strategic recommendations.

Job Description:
We are seeking a skilled Business Analyst to bridge the gap between business stakeholders and technical teams. You will analyze business processes, identify improvement opportunities, and help implement solutions.

Key Responsibilities:
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Analyze business processes and identify areas for improvement
- Create detailed functional specifications and user stories
- Facilitate meetings and workshops with stakeholders
- Develop process flow diagrams and documentation
- Conduct gap analysis and feasibility studies
- Support UAT (User Acceptance Testing) activities
- Create reports and dashboards for business metrics
- Collaborate with IT teams to ensure requirements are met
- Track project progress and communicate updates to stakeholders

Required Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Finance, or related field
- 2+ years of experience as a Business Analyst
- Strong analytical and critical thinking skills
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, and Visio
- Experience with SQL for data extraction and analysis
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Knowledge of business process modeling techniques
- Understanding of project management methodologies
- Ability to work effectively with diverse stakeholders

Preferred Qualifications:
- MBA or relevant certifications (CBAP, PMI-PBA)
- Experience with business intelligence tools (Tableau, Power BI)
- Knowledge of Agile and Scrum methodologies
- Familiarity with CRM and ERP systems
- Industry-specific domain knowledge
- Experience with requirements management tools (JIRA, Confluence)

What We Offer:
- Competitive salary and performance bonuses
- Professional certification support
- Exposure to diverse projects and industries
- Collaborative work environment
- Health and retirement benefits
- Work-life balance and flexible schedules
