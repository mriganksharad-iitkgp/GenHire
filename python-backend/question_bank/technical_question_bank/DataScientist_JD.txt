Job Title: Data Scientist

Company Overview:
We are a data-driven organization that leverages advanced analytics and machine learning to solve complex business problems and drive strategic decision-making.

Job Description:
We are seeking a talented Data Scientist to join our analytics team. You will work with large datasets to extract meaningful insights, build predictive models, and help shape our data strategy.

Key Responsibilities:
- Analyze large, complex datasets to identify trends, patterns, and insights
- Build and deploy machine learning models to solve business problems
- Create data visualizations and reports to communicate findings to stakeholders
- Collaborate with engineering teams to productionize models
- Design and conduct A/B tests and experiments
- Develop predictive models and recommendation systems
- Clean, preprocess, and validate data for analysis
- Stay current with latest developments in data science and ML

Required Qualifications:
- Master's or PhD in Statistics, Computer Science, Mathematics, or related field
- 2+ years of experience in data science or analytics role
- Strong proficiency in Python and R for data analysis
- Experience with machine learning frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn)
- Solid understanding of statistics and probability theory
- Proficiency in SQL and database querying
- Experience with data visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI, matplotlib, seaborn)
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Excellent communication skills to present technical concepts to non-technical audiences

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with big data technologies (Spark, Hadoop)
- Knowledge of deep learning and neural networks
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS SageMaker, Azure ML, Google AI Platform)
- Familiarity with MLOps practices
- Published research or contributions to data science community
- Domain expertise in specific industries (finance, healthcare, e-commerce)

What We Offer:
- Competitive compensation and comprehensive benefits
- Access to state-of-the-art computing resources
- Opportunities to work on challenging problems with real-world impact
- Collaborative team environment
- Professional development and conference attendance
- Flexible work arrangements
